Disconnect ?

Ratatosk will revoke this vendor’s site access. Its invoice list and duplicate protection stay available if you reconnect later.

Forget history?

Ratatosk will forget which invoices it delivered and remove them from this list. Existing files are not deleted, and the next sync may download them again.

Move to another destination?

Moving starts a fresh collection: every invoice Ratatosk can still reach is delivered again to the new destination. Invoices already delivered to the old one stay there and cannot be taken back.
